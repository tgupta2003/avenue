February

VC Panel: How Investors Decide

We kicked off 2026 with a powerful panel featuring Amie Patel (Elevar Equity), Aury M. Cifuentes (How Women Invest), and Zoe van den Bol (Alpha Partners), who brought distinct perspectives spanning impact-driven and revenue-focused investing. Despite their different theses and career stages, they aligned on backing strong founders, taking thoughtful risks, and the importance of women building expertise, networks, and ownership in venture. One key takeaway: if we want funding to shift, more women need to influence where capital flows — especially at the LP level.
