The Avenue VC Program 2025

This summer, we launched the inaugural Avenue VC Cohort with a clear mission: to create a space where undergraduate women could explore venture capital beyond what a Google search or textbook could offer.

Over the course of 8 weeks, we brought together powerful voices in the space who generously shared their time, expertise, and honest perspectives. Our cohort connected with the following amazing investors & speakers:


    * Zac Geinzer - Investor @ Commonweal Ventures Co-Founder Washington Square Angels

    * Will Mckelvey - Seed Investor @ Lerer Hippeau

    * Andrew Sheppard - Managing Director @ Transcend Fund

    * Garnet S. Heraman - Co-Founder & Managing Partner at Aperture® Venture Capital

    * Tanvi Lal - Investor @ Intuit Ventures

    * Diane Franchon - Director New Energy UK & SLB Corporate Ventures

    * Azade Hosseini - Investor @ NYU Innovation Fund


A Few Key Takeaways for Our Cohort

    * The importance of recognizing your personal work style

    * Gathering diverse perspectives and recognizing patterns helps investors act decisively and spot what is monetizable.

    * Networking is most powerful when it feels like building real friendships.

    * Having a unique perspective or even a “hot take” makes you memorable
