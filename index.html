The Avenue Venture Program

An 8-week summer program where undergraduate women meet investors and explore venture capital beyond the Google search.

"My biggest takeaway is realizing that the VC world is far more diverse and accessible than I once believed, having thought it was mostly gatekept for people from traditional finance or tech. Meeting investors across sectors and engaging deeply as a small cohort made the learning experience truly special!" - Nora He (NYU'27)

Behind the Build: Female Founders Panel & Workshop

An interactive session with founders Indianna Roehrich (Tomorrow-Today Beauty) and Trisha Goyal (BreakSports), where students got to ask questions and learn directly from founders about building in beauty and sports tech.
